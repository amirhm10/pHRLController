# Exclude Water and Combine Commanded and Calculated Flows

## Objective

Revise the Aug. 7 BioSMB figure package so water is excluded because no water
reservoir scale was used, and place the commanded and mass-calculated acid and
sodium acetate flows inside each combined pH/flow figure.

## Files changed

- `helpers/biosmb_experiment_data.py`
  - Added an explicit `include_water` option to the reusable stream mapping.
- `helpers/biosmb_experiment_plotting.py`
  - Made the combined layout depend on the selected stream count.
  - Added commanded and mass-calculated flow traces to each acid/base panel.
  - Added a full-range inset when a short scale transient would otherwise
    flatten the useful comparison.
  - Labeled the exact mass-difference interval and the meaning of negative
    calculated values.
- `analysis/plot_biosmb_experiment.py`
  - Made the reusable runner acid/base-only by default.
  - Added `--include-water` for a future experiment with a water scale.
  - Restricted exported tables, metrics, figures, densities, and manifest
    entries to selected streams.
- `tests/test_biosmb_experiment_plotting.py`
  - Covered the acid/base-only configuration and the updated combined plotting
    interfaces while retaining the explicit invalid-water test.

## Method and interpretation

For each stream, interval-average bottle-out flow remains

\[
F = \frac{m_{start} - m_{end}}{\rho}\frac{60}{\Delta t},
\]

with mass in grams, density in g/mL, time in seconds, and flow in mL/min.
Therefore, a negative calculated value means the recorded reservoir mass rose
during that interval. Adding solution to a weighed source bottle could cause
that sign, but a scale disturbance or asynchronous logging can also cause it.

The Aug. 7 evidence supports the short scale-disturbance explanation:

- 4-second acid calculation: 2 negative values among 4,096 intervals; minimum
  -2.689 mL/min.
- 4-second sodium acetate calculation: 3 negative values among 4,096
  intervals; minimum -81.542 mL/min.
- The largest sodium anomaly is paired with an immediate positive spike of
  +74.903 mL/min around 11:34:38-11:34:46 UTC.
- 60-second acid calculation: 0 negative values among 273 intervals.
- 60-second sodium acetate calculation: 0 negative values among 273 intervals.

Thus, the one-minute net measurements do not show a refill-like negative flow.
The rare 4-second sign reversals are retained in the tables and shown in the
seconds figure; they are not interpreted as physical reverse pump flow.

## Known limitations and next steps

- The 4-second derivative is sensitive to scale resolution, movement, and
  timestamp alignment; use the 60-second result for the main flow comparison.
- Density remains assumed to be 1.0 g/mL for both 100 mM solutions.
- The positive one-minute sodium bias should be checked with a calibration run
  using independently measured delivered volume or mass.
