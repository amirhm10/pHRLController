## Interpretation

The longer 50k run with 400-step setpoint holds improved the overall and late-training tracking metrics compared with the earlier 25k run. The policy performs very well after exploration decay, with late-training MAE near `0.0106` pH.

However, the final deterministic evaluation cycle had `0.0428` pH MAE, worse than the previous 25k run's final evaluation cycle. The final cycle target was `4.9776` pH, while the policy settled near `5.0207` pH, producing an approximately `0.043` pH offset. Since this is only one final held-out setpoint, it should not be treated as a robust generalization result.
