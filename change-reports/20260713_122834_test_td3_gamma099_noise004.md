# Test offline TD3 with gamma 0.99 and final noise 0.04

## Objective

Define the next controlled 500000-step offline TD3 experiment by changing the
discount factor from `0.97` to `0.99` and the final exploration standard
deviation from `0.02` to `0.04`, while preserving the successful network sizes,
batch size, reward, plant, action mapping, and seed.

## Files changed

- `run_offline_ph_td3_training.py`
  - changed the default `gamma` to `0.99`;
  - changed the default final exploration standard deviation to `0.04`.
- `tests/test_offline_ph_rl.py`
  - updated the default-setting regression assertions.
- `reports/offline_ph_td3_method_report.md`

    BioSMB shipment checkpoint.

## Method or implementation summary

The new default experiment uses:

- 500000 rollout steps;
- actor and critic layers `[128, 128]`;
- `gamma = 0.99`;
- batch size `64`;
- replay capacity `60000`;
- Gaussian exploration `0.35 -> 0.04`, linearly over 5000 actions;
- seed `7`;
- the unchanged `relative_band_offset` reward.

The TD3 one-step target now uses

$$
y_t = r_t + 0.99(1-d_t)\min_{i\in\{1,2\}}
Q_{\theta_i'}(s_{t+1},\tilde{a}_{t+1}).
$$

Compared with the successful `gamma = 0.97` baseline, the approximate discount
horizon increases from 33 to 100 decisions. Doubling the final exploration
standard deviation from `0.02` to `0.04` increases final exploration variance
by a factor of four. This may improve replay diversity, but it may also increase
training-action variation and critic-target variance.

The latest exact reproduction run,
`offline_ph_td3_training_20260713_034017`, reproduced all reported metrics from
`offline_ph_td3_training_20260710_183129` exactly. It therefore provides a
controlled local baseline for this experiment.
