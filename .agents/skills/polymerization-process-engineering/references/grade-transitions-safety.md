# Grade Transitions and Safety

## Transition metrics

- time to enter quality band
- total off-spec mass
- maximum temperature
- maximum heat-generation rate
- conversion trajectory
- \(M_n\), \(M_w\), and dispersity
- initiator and monomer use
- cooling saturation
- actuator movement
- fouling indicators
- final offset

## Scenario design

Test:

- nearby and wide grade changes
- feed and utility disturbances
- measurement delay
- kinetic uncertainty
- cooling loss
- actuator limits
- soft-sensor bias

## Safety distinction

A transition is not safe merely because final quality is acceptable. Evaluate the full transient and utility margins.
