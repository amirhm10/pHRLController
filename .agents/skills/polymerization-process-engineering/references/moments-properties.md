# Moments and Polymer Properties

## Moment consistency

Record:

- distribution represented
- live or dead chains
- number or mass basis
- truncation or closure
- end-group treatment
- repeat-unit mass
- branching variables

## Common properties

- number-average molecular weight
- weight-average molecular weight
- dispersity
- degree of polymerization
- composition
- branching index
- viscosity or melt index

## Checks

- \(\lambda_0,\lambda_1,\lambda_2\ge 0\)
- \(M_w\ge M_n\) under standard definitions
- units
- conservation
- closure sensitivity
- comparison with full distribution when available
- property-correlation range

A moment model can match low-order moments while missing tails important for rheology.
