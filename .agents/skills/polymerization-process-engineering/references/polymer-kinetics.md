# Polymer Kinetics

## Free-radical example families

- initiator decomposition
- radical initiation
- propagation
- termination by combination or disproportionation
- chain transfer to monomer, solvent, polymer, or agent
- inhibition
- branching

## Audit

- live and dead chain definitions
- radical concentration approximation
- initiator efficiency
- termination mechanism
- chain-transfer constants
- temperature dependence
- diffusion-controlled kinetics
- gel effect
- copolymer reactivity ratios
- catalyst activation and deactivation

## Units

Rate constants can have different dimensions depending on reaction order and concentration basis. Verify every rate term.
