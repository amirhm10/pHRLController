# Kinetics and Identifiability

## Rate audit

- species and stoichiometry
- rate law
- reaction order
- basis
- units
- temperature dependence
- equilibrium
- inhibition
- catalyst
- transport correction

## Structural identifiability

Ask whether unique parameters could be recovered from ideal noise-free observations under the proposed experiment.

## Practical identifiability

Inspect:

- parameter covariance
- profile likelihood
- sensitivity matrix
- condition number
- correlation
- confidence intervals
- dependence on initial guess
- out-of-sample prediction

## Experiment design

Excite:

- temperature
- concentration
- residence time
- relevant catalyst or initiator conditions

without violating safety or leaving the model's physical domain.
