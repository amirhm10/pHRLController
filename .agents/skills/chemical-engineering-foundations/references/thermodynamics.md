# Thermodynamics Audit

## Questions

- Which phases are possible?
- Is equilibrium assumed?
- Which property method is used?
- Are fugacity or activity coefficients needed?
- Are binary or electrolyte parameters available?
- Is the pressure range compatible?
- Are enthalpy and heat-capacity references consistent?
- Are pseudocomponents or polymers present?
- Could phase splitting or azeotropy occur?

## Common model families

- ideal gas and ideal solution
- cubic equations of state
- activity-coefficient models
- electrolyte activity models
- association models
- polymer-specific frameworks

Select based on system physics, not convenience.

## Phase equilibrium

A generic equality of component fugacity is

\[
f_i^\alpha(T,P,\mathbf{x}^\alpha)
=
f_i^\beta(T,P,\mathbf{x}^\beta).
\]

The \(K\)-value form \(y_i=K_i x_i\) is a representation, not a universal constant relationship.

## Validation

Compare against:

- known boiling points or phase behavior
- trusted property data
- limiting ideal behavior
- mass and energy balances
- sensitivity to method or parameters
