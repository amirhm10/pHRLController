# Balances and Units

## Balance template

For any conserved quantity:

\[
\text{accumulation}
=
\text{in}
-
\text{out}
+
\text{generation}
-
\text{consumption}.
\]

## Checklist

- control volume
- basis
- sign convention
- phase and component
- reaction generation
- storage
- units
- time basis
- reference state
- closure tolerance

## Common unit traps

- kg/h versus kmol/h
- mol/L versus kmol/m\(^3\)
- Celsius used in Arrhenius expressions
- bar gauge versus bar absolute
- standard versus actual cubic meters
- kW versus kJ/h
- mass-based versus molar heat capacity
- per-reactor-volume versus per-catalyst-mass rate

## Data reconciliation

When measurements do not close balances:

- preserve raw measurements
- record sensor uncertainty
- formulate weighted reconciliation
- identify gross errors
- do not force exact closure without reporting adjustments
