# Transport and Reaction Checks

## Reaction

- balanced stoichiometry
- rate basis
- rate-limiting step
- reversible or irreversible
- Arrhenius temperature
- catalyst activity
- inhibition
- equilibrium limit
- heat of reaction
- parameter validity range

## Transport

- mixing assumption
- heat-transfer coefficient and area
- mass-transfer coefficient and area
- pressure drop
- residence time
- diffusion
- convection
- interphase transfer
- wall or jacket dynamics

## Time-scale comparison

Compare characteristic time scales:

- reaction
- mixing
- heat transfer
- mass transfer
- residence
- sensor
- actuator
- controller

A lumped equilibrium model is questionable when transport is not fast relative to reaction or control.
