# Safe Operating Envelope

For each critical variable, record:

| Variable | Normal | Alarm | Trip | Design or damage | Units | Basis and source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Include

- steady and transient limits
- rate limits
- coupled limits
- duration dependence
- uncertainty
- sensor range
- actuator authority
- utility margin
- model-validity range

A rectangular box may be inadequate when limits are coupled, such as pressure-temperature or vapor-liquid traffic.
